What drives me

Where I'm headed, and why I keep showing up to ICT events.

I like turning a vague idea into something you can actually click through. That's most of the appeal of programming for me. I'm drawn to projects that fix a specific, real annoyance: a queue that's actually fair, a records app that doesn't make a patient dig through five menus just to book a check-up.

Outside of class I keep showing up to ICT and cybersecurity talks run by DICT, DOST, and local provincial offices. Not because I have to, but because I want a running sense of where the industry is heading before I graduate into it. AI, digital trust, and secure systems are the topics I keep gravitating back to.

The TESDA courses added the other half: knowing how to install, configure and maintain the systems that software actually runs on.
